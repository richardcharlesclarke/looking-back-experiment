# Study One: facilitator guide

**Help the same attendees answer a questionnaire before and after Big Brue, so we can see whether their approach to conflict, connection and understanding changes.**

[Participant questionnaire](https://experiments.evolvable.me/study-one) · [Preview for organisers — no responses submitted](https://experiments.evolvable.me/study-one?preview=1)

## What are we trying to learn?

The hypothesis is that festival experiences may help people see conflict as more constructive, become more willing to engage with different views, and develop a broader understanding of an issue—even when their position does not change. Study One also asks about felt connection and outlook.

We compare each person’s answers before and after the festival and ask what they experienced. This reveals reported changes and associations; it cannot establish that the festival caused them. [Study overview](https://www.notion.so/3d07d52ecd5481128df1c7210c427521), [discussion of the intended outcomes](https://www.notion.so/3d67d52ecd5481298f6dd11f36ee1d86).

## What happens?

**Invitation → before questionnaire → attend normally → personal email link → after questionnaire → compare matched answers**

1. **Before attending any festival activities:** an attendee aged 18 or over reads the study information, chooses whether to participate and completes the first questionnaire. The published estimate is about eight minutes; allow a little breathing room.
2. **After saving those answers:** they may separately provide an email address and agree to receive the second questionnaire. This is optional. The optional Evolvable link at the end is also entirely separate from taking part.
3. **During the festival:** they attend normally. Study One requires no special briefing, assigned panel or facilitated conversation. The panel-briefing intervention belongs to Study Two.
4. **After the festival:** the research assistant emails each consenting participant their own link. It automatically matches the second questionnaire to the first—no code to remember. Participants revisit the measures and selected topic/view, and report attendance and what, if anything, they understood differently.
5. **The study team:** compares matched answers, keeping the different outcomes separate and noting missing follow-ups.

## Organiser checklist

**Before invitations go out**

- Confirm who distributes the invitation, who sends follow-ups and who monitors replies. The current study contact is **beau@labofmisfits.com**; confirm authorised sending access and inbox cover.
- Share the participant link above through the agreed festival channel. If using a QR code, point it to that link. Use the preview link only for rehearsal.
- Schedule completion before attendees encounter festival activities. The current implementation closes the first questionnaire at **10:00 BST on Saturday 19 September 2026** and opens the second at **00:00 BST on Monday 21 September 2026**. Confirm these configured times fit the invitation plan, including Sunday-only attendees.
- Rehearse on a phone in preview mode; check connectivity and the programme choices. Do not enter practice answers into the live study.

**When helping attendees**

- Explain the two questionnaires and optional email choice. Let people read the information and consent themselves.
- Help with navigation, without suggesting answers or telling people they should become more open-minded. People can skip questions or stop.
- Remind those opting in to check their email address and keep their personal link private.

**After the festival**

- The research assistant opens the [protected assistant workspace](https://study-one-review-study-one-review.up.railway.app/admin/brufest/festival), checks the saved first response and email permission, and copies the correct personal follow-up draft into the agreed email system. Exclude demonstration records.
- Send each link only to its saved recipient. Record it as sent externally only after sending; record failures separately. **The website does not send emails.**
- For a lost link, check the request and resend to the saved address. Direct withdrawal or other participant requests to the study contact. Keep contact lists and personal links private.
