# Brufest paired discussion study — helper run sheet

Local review version, 7 September 2026. This describes the working revised prototype and a proposed helper-led festival procedure. It is not permission to collect real participant data.

## What are we doing this for?

We want to test whether a short briefing helps two people who disagree engage with each other’s reasons, understand each other and produce useful new distinctions or questions.

Compare pairs given the disagreement briefing with pairs given an logistical briefing in the same five-minute slot. Both members of a pair get the same briefing. Both groups then do the same timed discussion and questionnaires. A better discussion does not require agreement, moving towards the middle or lower confidence. This is a pilot hypothesis, not an established effect.

## What the helper needs

- A helper to invite volunteers, read screening responses and form pairs; someone to deliver the assigned briefing and time each discussion. One person may cover these jobs if the schedule allows. Named staff, recruitment location and capacity are still decisions.
- The approved participant information, consent, withdrawal and recording procedure. These are not yet supplied. Explain participation identically in both conditions before screening. People may skip questions or stop. The prototype does not collect consent and must not be used for real responses yet.
- One private questionnaire device per participant, or a carefully managed shared device; a helper device with access to the organiser workspace. Keep participants’ private answers out of their partner’s view.
- A quiet place where a pair can hear its briefing without exposing other pairs to it; a consistent way to record both openings, discussion and joint-output stage with participants’ permission. The website does not record the discussion.
- A saved personal return link for each participant. A volunteer number helps the helper locate the person; it is not a password or a substitute for the private link.
- A tested public address before festival use. The current localhost address works only on this Mac, including when a link is opened in a different browser on this Mac. It is not usable from a volunteer’s phone at the festival.

## 1. Invite volunteer A and give the screening questionnaire

The helper explains the activity using the approved information sheet, checks willingness to be screened, and gives the direct Participant screening questionnaire link. No pair or conversation record needs to exist yet.

The questionnaire asks about three topics from the festival programme: migration, assisted dying (from Family Matters), and AI. For each, A gives a position, personal importance, an optional reason, and willingness to discuss it with someone who sees it differently. Skipping is explicit. Repeat with volunteer B and other volunteers.

The current statements are new draft wording for Richard’s review. The organiser workspace shows exact statements, sources and reasoning. They are not quotations approved by the speakers or organisers. Review scope, balance and comprehensibility, and time the screening, before locking a research version.

## 2. Each volunteer keeps a personal return link

After A saves screening, the page shows A’s volunteer number and buttons to copy the personal return link or save it as a file. A keeps this link and shows the number to the helper. A does not memorise or type a long code. The browser also retains the link locally when browser storage works.

The same link opens A’s next questionnaire as the helper advances the activity. It can be reopened in a different browser or on another device when served from a reachable address. It grants access to A’s questionnaires, so A keeps it private. B has a different link.

On a shared device, save A’s link before using “Start screening for another volunteer”. To return to A, open A’s saved link. Draft answers are stored in the browser until submitted; they do not transfer to another device. Submitted answers remain on the server.

If browser storage is lost, use the saved link. The helper can copy the same personal link for a volunteer whose saved receipt establishes the correct number. If the receipt, link and browser data are all lost, do not guess an identity or silently match by a name. Record the loss and follow the agreed recovery/withdrawal procedure. Automatic identity recovery is not implemented.

## 3. The helper forms one pair on one topic

In the helper workspace, read each volunteer’s screening position, importance, reason and willingness. Choose two people who both said yes to the same topic and whose views differ meaningfully. The software rejects identical positions, missing positions, unwilling volunteers and people already paired. It does not decide whether the disagreement is substantive; the helper reads the reasons and checks with both people.

Proposed consistent rule for review: take the earliest suitable waiting volunteers and the first suitable topic in the displayed order. Prefer unfamiliar partners; familiarity is recorded in the before questionnaire. Record the reason for the pairing and any departure from the rule. Do not pick people because they are expected to respond well to the intervention. Do not claim that this rule or a numeric distance has been validated.

Select A, B and the topic, write why they are a suitable pair, and create the pair. Both are linked to the identities created at screening. No new code or private-key entry is needed. One person cannot join a second pair.

## 4. A and B privately complete the before questionnaire

A and B reopen their own personal links. Each receives Questionnaire before the discussion. They answer about their current views, their reasons, their understanding of the opposing view and their prediction of their partner’s position. Prior briefing/study exposure and familiarity are recorded. Screening does not replace this baseline.

The helper checks that both before questionnaires are saved. The software blocks allocation until both are complete. If someone cannot continue, use the early-stop procedure; do not invent missing answers.

## 5. Allocate and deliver the briefing

The helper clicks Allocate briefing and begin. Only now does the software randomly assign the pair, with a 50:50 chance of either briefing. It saves a reproducible seed, assignment time, method, opening order and exact briefing text. Assignment is concealed from the participants’ pages. Allocation is independent per pair; topic-stratified balancing is not implemented. If stratification is required, specify it before collection.

Read the displayed script to both people, separately from other pairs. The disagreement script asks them to reflect privately on uncertainty in their own position, consider a genuine question, represent another person’s strongest reasons before responding, and qualify claims if warranted. The control script covers timing, questionnaire links and logistics without that coaching. Its logistics have been adapted to the personal-link workflow and versioned separately.

Aim for five minutes including reflection or logistical review. Rehearse the actual delivery first. The helper timer records elapsed wall time, including pauses; log duration deviations and interruptions. No extra coaching should be improvised in one condition.

## 6. Run the same discussion task in both groups

The software chooses the first speaker independently of condition. The helper sees whose opening comes first.

- First participant: two uninterrupted minutes to explain their position and main reasons.
- Second participant: two uninterrupted minutes for their position and main reasons.
- Both participants: eight minutes to discuss the statement together. The helper announces the start and end and does not coach them towards agreement.
- Both participants: three minutes to complete one Joint discussion record on A’s device. This opens only after the discussion stage. Ask what the disagreement turns on, each person’s concern and any new uncertainty/question/proposal. “None identified” and different descriptions are valid.

Use the stage controls as these activities happen. The app shows time remaining and records actual duration when the helper advances. A deviation over 30 seconds needs a note; this logs rather than conceals interruptions or a fast demonstration. Give joint-record prompts after the discussion, not before. Code discussion and prompted joint output separately.

The briefing plus timed task takes approximately 20 minutes. This excludes invitation, screening, pairing, before/after questionnaires and transitions. Full duration has not yet been measured.

## 7. Private after questionnaire, then partner check

After the joint record is saved, the helper opens the private after questionnaires. A and B use their own links. Repeated measures use the same statement, wording and scale as before. Additional items ask about actual engagement, respect, qualification, useful output and willingness to continue.

Only once both after questionnaires are saved does each see the other person’s description of their reason. A privately rates how accurately B described A’s reason; B does the reverse. Neither receives the partner’s other private answers. This is a post-only accuracy assessment, not a before/after accuracy change score.

The helper checks both partner ratings, closes the activity and uses the approved explanation/debrief. Do not provide invented scores or personality feedback.

## If someone stops

Stop the discussion immediately if asked. The helper records an interruption or withdrawal without unnecessary identifying details and chooses End activity with incomplete questionnaires. Existing records, assignment and missingness remain visible in exports. Removing already submitted research data requires the separately agreed withdrawal/retention procedure; ending the activity does not delete data.

## What gets compared?

Compare the randomly assigned groups at the pair level. Each pair is one independently allocated unit; two people or many speaking turns do not create extra independent pairs.

Keep these distinct: engagement with the other person’s reasons; private understanding change; accuracy of predicting the partner’s position; the partner’s post-only rating of their reason summary; useful new output in the joint record; willingness to continue; and independently coded behaviour from recordings. Participant-reported novelty and independently coded novelty are different evidence. Do not call any change of position inherently better.

The software exports raw item responses, exact wording, instrument version, pair/topic, assignment, timing history, missing answers and demonstration markers. Exported data excludes personal-link secrets. Analyse people as assigned and report missing responses, early stops, prior exposure and missing recordings by condition. No automatic outcome score, transcript analysis or adequate-power claim is provided.
