Five descriptive bands cover [0,20), [20,40), [40,60), [60,80) and [80,100]. Labels describe a continuous position and cannot snap it to a category centre.

Legacy seven-point submissions remain in their original versions and raw values. Requests from already-open legacy pages without a version still validate against the legacy scale. Research exports expose instrument version, response type, bounds and bands. The admin view identifies legacy versus continuous responses. Matched before/after exports retain both raw values but mark mixed scales as not directly comparable. Question IDs, wording and order, consent, contact permission, matching, date gates, retention and storage remain unchanged.
