# Study One response-scale review — 10 September 2026

Richard authorised the item-by-item review, implementation and production deployment. New instrument: `brufest-study-one-item-scales-v5-2026-09-10`. Approved wording, item IDs, ordering, conditional rules, numeric bounds, reverse directions, and continuous slider interaction are preserved. V4 and all earlier instruments are frozen; existing enrolments and baselines keep their matching follow-up. Changed labels constitute a new instrument, with no silent conversion of historical values.

Fourteen core scales change explicitly: six agreement, three willingness, one desire and four perceived ability. The two likelihood scales, three connection circles, topic certainty and willingness scales, conditional keynote impact scale, and every categorical/text response remain unchanged. Agreement on negatively phrased items preserves their original direction. No psychometric validation is claimed.

## Complete item audit

Every rendered question is listed below for each questionnaire, including all 26 attendance branches and the conditional topic/learning questions. The selected-topic questions appear only with a selected panel and written starting view; post-festival ratings reference the saved baseline. Exposure rows appear only for each selected session; impact only after All/Part keynote attendance; learning detail only after Yes to encountering another view.
