# Study 3 — Small-group disagreement trial • v0.1

## Research question and status
Does the same short briefing improve substantive engagement, understanding and useful new output in a standardised disagreement between festival attendees?
Included in the pilot. Bring together two attendees who disagree, try the briefing, and see what their conversation produces. Working questions and instructions: v0.1, 5 September 2026. Fill in location, topics and helpers later.
## Unit and default format
Use pairs for v0.1: two attendees who genuinely disagree, one topic, one conversation per person. This removes pair/trio differences in airtime and interaction structure. Trios remain a later variant and must not be mixed into the pair analysis without a separately specified protocol.
The pair is the randomisation unit. Both members receive the same condition. Participants may also be in Study 1 or panel audiences; record prior study participation and exposure. Do not call repeated participation independent.
## Screening and pairing
- E3_SCREEN_POSITION — To what extent do you support [candidate proposition]? [0 Strongly oppose → 10 Strongly support]. Repeat across the configured neutral proposition bank.
- E3_SCREEN_IMPORTANCE — How important is this issue to you personally? [0 Not at all → 10 Extremely]
- E3_SCREEN_REASON — What is the main reason for your position? [Short text]
- E3_SCREEN_WILLING — Would you be willing to discuss this issue with someone who sees it differently? [Yes; No]
- E3_SCREEN_KNOWN — Do you already know the person you have been paired with? [No; Yes; Prefer not to answer]
Pair people who are both willing and whose positions and reasons reveal a real difference they want to discuss. Match on the first available suitable proposition using a recorded rule; do not select on expected treatment response. Prefer unfamiliar partners for the standard task; flag familiar pairs for separate description.
Proposition bank placeholder: [topic ID; specific neutral claim; 0 Strongly oppose; 10 Strongly support]. No invented final festival topics. Screen before pairing; screening positions do not replace the immediate PRE measures.
## Allocation and sequence
Assign participant tokens and pair ID. Collect private PRE from both participants before briefing. Then randomly assign each pair 1:1 to treatment or active control, using a stored random seed and assignment log. Stratify by topic where numbers permit; do not change assignments after outcomes. Both members receive their assigned briefing separately from other pairs.
Flow: screening → pairing → private PRE → pair allocation → treatment/control briefing → common task instructions → two-minute opening each → eight-minute conversation → three-minute joint output → private POST and partner assessment.
Record actual durations. Randomise which participant opens first independently of condition. Use one session per participant. Record prior briefing, keynote, intervention-panel or small-group exposure before allocation, and report contamination separately.
## Private PRE and repeated POST
Intro: “Thinking about this issue and the person you are discussing it with, how true is each statement of you now?” Scale 1 Not at all true of me now → 7 Very true of me now; separate Prefer not to answer.
- E3_OPN_01 — My current position on this issue may be incomplete.
- E3_CUR_01 — I am interested in understanding how the other person’s position makes sense to them.
- E3_REG_01 — I regard the other person’s reasons as worth examining carefully.
- E3_REV_01 — I am willing to qualify a claim if the evidence or argument warrants it.
- E3_DEF_01 — I can be challenged on this issue without treating the challenge as personal.
- E3_AGY_01 — I feel able to help this disagreement remain productive.
Repeat these exact proposition measures privately at PRE and POST:
- E3_POSITION — To what extent do you support [proposition]? [0 Strongly oppose → 10 Strongly support]
- E3_CONFIDENCE — How confident are you in your position on [proposition]? [0 Not at all confident → 10 Completely confident]
- E3_UNDERSTANDING — How well do you understand the strongest reasons for a position different from your own on [proposition]? [0 Not at all → 10 Extremely well]
- E3_PREDICT — Where do you think your partner stands on [proposition]? [Same position scale; Cannot estimate]
- E3_OTHER_REASON — What do you think is your partner’s strongest reason for their position? [Short text; I do not yet know]
- E3_OWN_REASON — What is your strongest reason for your position? [Short text]
PRE exposure: E3_PRIOR — Have you already received a disagreement briefing or joined another study conversation at this festival? [No; Yes; Not sure; details]. Record keynote and relevant panel attendance by session IDs.
## Common task instructions: both conditions
“You will discuss [proposition]. You each have two uninterrupted minutes to explain your position and your main reasons. [Participant A/B] will start. After both openings, you have eight minutes to discuss the issue together. You do not have to agree. After the conversation, you will have three minutes to complete a short joint record. You may skip a question or stop participating at any time.”
Both groups receive these same instructions. Give the joint-output prompts only after the eight-minute conversation so they do not differentially coach the discussion. Record the full task consistently; code the discussion and joint-output stages separately.
## Joint output: same prompts in both conditions
- E3_JOINT_TURN — What is the main point on which your disagreement turns?
- E3_JOINT_A — What is an important concern behind Participant A’s position?
- E3_JOINT_B — What is an important concern behind Participant B’s position?
- E3_JOINT_UNCERTAINTY — Did you identify an uncertainty that was new to either of you? Describe it, or write “None identified”.
- E3_JOINT_NEW — Did you produce a new question, distinction or conditional proposal? Describe it, or write “None identified”.
“Record differences if you cannot agree on the description. A shared conclusion is not required.” Do not force a positive output or score completion itself as generativity. Compare claimed novelty with openings and PRE reasons; participant-reported novelty and independently coded novelty are separate fields.
## Private POST-only questions
Short-form default: POST_01, POST_03, POST_04, POST_05, POST_06 and POST_07, followed by the partner accuracy rating. CHANGE, POST_02, POST_08, POST_09 and GENERATED are optional extensions.
Scale 1 Strongly disagree → 7 Strongly agree; separate Cannot assess and Prefer not to answer.
- E3_POST_01 — The other person responded to the reasons I gave.
- E3_POST_02 — The other person asked questions to understand my view.
- E3_POST_03 — The other person represented my view accurately.
- E3_POST_04 — I felt respected while being challenged.
- E3_POST_05 — I refined or qualified a claim because of something the other person said.
- E3_POST_06 — The conversation produced a distinction, question or proposal that was not available at the start.
- E3_POST_07 — I would willingly continue this conversation with this person.
- E3_POST_08 — The exchange became more about winning than understanding. [Adverse direction]
- E3_POST_09 — We avoided an important part of our disagreement. [Adverse direction]
- E3_CHANGE — What best describes any change in your thinking about this issue? [Shared topic-change choices; maximum two]
- E3_GENERATED — What, if anything, did the conversation make possible that was not available at the start? [Optional text]
## Partner assessment
After each person completes their own POST, show them the partner's POST summary of their reason, identified by participant label rather than contact details.
- E3_ACCURACY — How accurately does this summary capture your strongest reason? [0 Not at all accurately → 10 Very accurately; Cannot assess]
- E3_CORRECTION — What, if anything, is missing or inaccurate? [Optional text]
Collect assessments privately before any joint debrief. The main accuracy rating is post-only, not an accuracy-change score. PRE reason summaries are retained for blinded coding; self-rated understanding is a separate repeated outcome.
## Outcomes and analysis
Look for direct engagement, better understanding and useful new output. Use the recording, joint record and private answers together; keep discussion behaviour separate from the answers prompted by the joint-record form.
Key secondary outcomes: independently coded joint-output novelty/integration; mean partner accuracy rating; mean within-person understanding change; position-prediction error change; willingness to continue. Use pair-level summaries and report both partners' availability; do not treat two people as two independently randomised units. Position and confidence changes remain descriptive.
Analyse as assigned, retain records of briefing noncompletion, interruptions and missing recordings, and show missingness by condition. Topic-stratified allocation must be reflected in analysis. Sample target and detectable effect remain placeholders; do not promise significance or large effects from a brief intervention.
